How TruthGuard Works

TruthGuard combines AI-based text analysis with real-time news verification to help you identify misleading or fake news.

1

User Submits News

The user pastes a news headline, article, or URL into the Analyze page.

2

AI Language Analysis

The system analyzes the text for bias, sensational language, and misleading patterns.

3

Source Verification

The news source is checked against a database of trusted and untrusted publishers.

4

Similar News Matching

Related articles are fetched from trusted news APIs and compared for relevance.

5

Credibility Score Generation

A final credibility score is calculated using weighted factors like source trust and content similarity.

Credibility Score Logic

Trusted Source

+40 points

Similar Articles Found

+25 points

Neutral Language

+20 points

Sensational Language

-20 points
